Super Brainrot Z Italian Power

Who is the best Brainrot? Let's find out!

You saw it on TikTok — people choosing who beats who. Wouldn't it be great to settle it with the whole community by making them battle their way up a ranking? That's what I thought, and I had to make this game. I hope you like it.

About the game

This game was born after seeing lots of stories where people made versus between characters. Wouldn't it be better if we could actually make them compete? Each player picks their favorite, and every win gives their character a point in the ranking. Simple, community-driven and a little chaotic.

Join the community, vote for your favorite Brainrot and help it climb to the top. The ranking updates as the community plays — every vote counts!
