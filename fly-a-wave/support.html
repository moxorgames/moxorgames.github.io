Frequently asked questions

Where can I download the game?

Fly-A-Wave is available on Google Play and the App Store. Use the badges on the game page and you'll jump right to your store.

The game doesn't work on my device, what do I do?

Make sure your device is supported (Android 8+ / iOS 15+ recommended) and that the OS is updated. Restart the app; if it keeps failing, write to us with your device model and OS version.

How do I report a bug or a suggestion?

Write to moxorgames@gmail.com — include a screenshot or a short video if you can. I read every message.

Are there in-app purchases or ads?

No. Fly-A-Wave is ad-free and has no in-app purchases. Just you and the wave.

Can I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Reach out to their support for a refund; if there's a technical problem on our side we'll do our best to fix it quickly.
